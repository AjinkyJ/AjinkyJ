## 🔑 Key Projects

### 📡 BLE 5.0 Radiation Dosimeter — Hub & Peripheral
`STM32L162` · `Microchip RN4871` · `BLE 4.2` · `DMA` · `HAL`

Developed a BLE-based embedded system for radiation monitoring with modular HAL drivers and DMA-driven PIN diode acquisition over UART, featuring low-power sleep mode operation.

---

### 🔬 Portable NIR Spectroscopy — Plastic Identification
`STM32WB55` · `InGaAs Detector` · `Fixed-Point DSP` · `BLE` · `Deep Sleep`

Architected firmware for a portable NIR spectroscopy system for plastic composition detection (PET, HDPE, PVC). Implemented DMA-driven acquisition, real-time fixed-point DSP, and BLE connectivity with deep sleep modes for field-deployable operation.

---

### 💡 Fluorescence Intensity Measurement Device
`ESP32` · `SiPM` · `ADC` · `Digital Filtering` · `UART/I2C`

Developed an ADC and digital filtering pipeline for SiPM-based photon detection. Engineered a fixed curve-fitting calibration method with multi-protocol data transmission (UART & I2C).

---

### 🔋 Smart Gas AMR — Ultra Low Power
`Vega Aries MCU` · `GSM` · `Interrupt-Driven` · `Li-SOCl₂`

Designed an interrupt-driven Smart Gas AMR with duty-cycled GSM transmission and aggressive sleep management, projecting a **2-year battery life** on a 1200 mAh Li-SOCl₂ cell.
